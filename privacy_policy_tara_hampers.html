Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 10, 2026 | Application ID: com.friendstech.tarahampers

Welcome to Tara Hampers ("we," "our," or "us"), operated by FriendsTech. Your privacy is critically important to us. This Privacy Policy governs your use of the Tara Hampers mobile application for Android devices.

Summary: We collect minimal user information (such as display name, phone number, and delivery address for winner shipping) strictly to facilitate giveaway entries and hamper prize delivery. We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

1. Information We Collect

When you register or use Tara Hampers, we may collect the following types of information:

  • Account Information: Display Name, Phone Number, Email Address, and Avatar selection provided via Google Sign-In or Phone OTP Authentication.
  • Delivery Address: Shipping address provided by users when entering giveaways or claiming won hamper prizes.
  • Activity & Entries Data: Giveaway tickets earned, daily check-ins, rewarded ad views, and referral logs.
  • Device Identifiers: Anonymous device IDs, IP address, OS version, and Advertising ID used for Google AdMob and notification delivery via Firebase Cloud Messaging.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information exclusively for the following purposes:

  • To manage user authentication and keep your account secure.
  • To verify giveaway entries and execute fair winner selection draws.
  • To contact winners and ship physical hampers to their provided delivery addresses.
  • To deliver rewarded video advertisements and daily bonus tickets.
  • To send push notifications regarding giveaway draws, winners, and special hamper drops.

3. Third-Party Services We Use

Tara Hampers integrates with trusted third-party services that process data in accordance with their privacy policies:

  • Firebase Authentication & Messaging (Google): Used for secure user login, OTP verification, and push notifications. Google Privacy Policy
  • Supabase: Used for secure cloud database hosting and giveaway entry synchronization. Supabase Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob: Used for serving rewarded video ads. AdMob may collect device identifiers to serve personalized or non-personalized ads. AdMob Behavioral Policies
  • Cloudinary: Used for secure image uploads and asset hosting.

4. Data Retention & Security

We implement strict administrative, technical, and physical security measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, loss, or alteration. Account and entry data are retained as long as your account is active or needed to provide services.

5. Children's Privacy

Tara Hampers does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us immediately so we can remove the information.

6. User Data & Account Deletion Rights

You have the right to request access to or deletion of your personal data at any time. If you wish to delete your Tara Hampers account or remove your shipping address history, please contact us at friendstechs@gmail.com.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. We will notify you of any material changes by updating the effective date at the top of this policy.
