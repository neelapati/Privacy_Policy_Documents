1. Introduction

Welcome to Fake GPS Location ("the App"), developed by Friends Tech. We respect your privacy and are committed to full transparency regarding data collection, system permissions, advertising, and billing practices. This Privacy Policy details how user information is handled when you use the App.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

We operate under a strict user-privacy-first principle:

  • No Personal Identifiable Information (PII): We do not collect or store your name, email address, phone number, contacts, or hardware serial numbers on any private servers.
  • No Tracking or Server-Side Location Storage: Your real physical location and your simulated mock coordinates are processed entirely on your device and are never sent to or stored on our servers.
  • No Account Required: You can use all core features anonymously without registration or account creation.

3. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

All data you create remains securely stored within your device's isolated local storage:

  • Favorites & History: Saved target coordinates, custom pinpoints, and search history.
  • User Preferences: Travel speed, drift/jitter options, map view configurations, and custom preferences.
  • Local VIP Status: Locally cached subscription receipts or rewarded session timer status.

You can erase all locally stored data at any time by clearing the App data under Android Settings or uninstalling the App.

4. In-App Purchases and Subscriptions (Google Play Billing)

Payment Security: All financial transactions, one-time lifetime purchases, and auto-renewing subscriptions are processed exclusively and securely by Google Play In-App Billing.

Our app offers optional premium upgrades (monthly subscriptions, annual subscriptions, and one-time lifetime access):

  • No Financial Data Collection: We do not collect, process, or store credit card, debit card, UPI credentials, bank details, or billing addresses. All billing operations are handled through your Google Play account.
  • Subscription Management & Cancellation: Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled. You can manage, pause, or cancel your subscription at any time by visiting Google Play Store > Profile > Payments & Subscriptions > Subscriptions.
  • For more information on Google Play's billing policies, visit the Google Privacy Policy.

5. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version of the App displays ads provided by Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) to support ongoing maintenance and server tile costs:

  • Ad Types: The App contains standard banner ads, occasional interstitial ads, and user-initiated rewarded ads (which grant free temporary VIP access).
  • Information Processed by AdMob: The Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process device identifiers (such as the Google Advertising ID), coarse location, and general interaction data for fraud prevention, diagnostics, and delivering personalized or non-personalized ads in accordance with Google's policies.
  • Opt-Out: You can reset or opt out of personalized advertising at any time on your Android device by going to Settings > Google > Ads > Reset or Delete advertising ID.
  • To learn how Google manages advertising data, visit the Google Advertising Privacy Policy.

6. Device Permissions and Usage

The App requests only the permissions strictly necessary to simulate location and provide overlay controls:

A. Location Access (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION, ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION)

Used to center the map on your initial area when the App starts. Real GPS coordinates are processed solely in memory on your device and are never shared or uploaded.

B. Mock Location (ACCESS_MOCK_LOCATION & Developer Options)

Required by Android to register the App as the designated mock location provider under Android Developer Options ("Select mock location app"). This permits injecting simulated coordinates into the Android framework.

C. Foreground Service (FOREGROUND_SERVICE, FOREGROUND_SERVICE_LOCATION, POST_NOTIFICATIONS)

Required to sustain GPS mocking while using other navigation apps, testing apps, or when the screen is turned off. A persistent notification is always displayed in your notification tray indicating active simulation with a quick one-tap Stop button.

D. System Alert Window (SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW)

Used solely for the optional Floating Joystick Overlay, allowing you to walk, drive, or teleport across simulated coordinates while keeping other applications visible.

E. Network Access (INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE)

Required to fetch OpenStreetMap map tiles, perform address searches via OpenStreetMap Nominatim, serve Google AdMob ads, and verify purchase receipts with Google Play.

7. Third-Party Map Service (OpenStreetMap)

The App uses OpenStreetMap and Nominatim for map rendering and geocoding search queries. Map tile requests are anonymous. For details, refer to the OpenStreetMap Foundation Privacy Policy.

8. Children's Privacy

Fake GPS Location is designed for general audiences and developers, and is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Google Play Developer Policy Compliance

This Privacy Policy strictly adheres to the Google Play Developer Program Policies, including the Location Data Policy, Foreground Service Location Policy, In-App Billing Policy, and Google Play Data Safety requirements.
