1. Introduction

Welcome to Fake GPS Location ("the App"), developed by Friends Tech. We respect your privacy and are committed to maintaining the highest level of transparency and data security. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding user information when you use our mobile application.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

We operate under a strict zero-data-collection policy:

  • No Personal Identifiable Information (PII): We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, contacts, or device hardware IDs.
  • No Tracking or Server-Side Logging: Your physical location and your simulated mock coordinates are never transmitted to, stored on, or shared with external servers.
  • No Account Required: You can access all app features completely anonymously without signing in.

3. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

All data generated through your usage of the App remains strictly on your device:

  • Waypoints & Routes: Custom route points, GPX tracks, and simulated routes are saved locally in private app storage.
  • Favorites & History: Bookmarked places and recent searches remain private on your phone.
  • User Settings: Custom travel speeds, jitter/drift preferences, and map view configurations are retained locally.

You can erase all saved data at any time by clearing the App's data in Android Settings or uninstalling the App.

4. Device Permissions and Usage

To provide mock location services, the App requests specific system permissions:

A. Location Access (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION, ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION)

Used solely to determine your initial location on the map view when opening the App. Coordinates are processed entirely in memory on your device and are never sent externally.

B. Mock Location (ACCESS_MOCK_LOCATION & Developer Options)

Android requires declaring ACCESS_MOCK_LOCATION and selecting Fake GPS Location under Developer Options ("Select mock location app") to allow injecting mock coordinates into the device system.

C. Foreground Service (FOREGROUND_SERVICE, FOREGROUND_SERVICE_LOCATION, POST_NOTIFICATIONS)

Required to maintain location simulation while the screen is locked or while using other applications. A persistent system notification is displayed showing the active simulation status and offering a one-tap Stop button.

D. System Alert Window (SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW)

Used only for the optional Floating Joystick Overlay feature, allowing you to control simulated movements on top of other applications with your explicit consent.

E. Internet (INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE)

Required to load OpenStreetMap tiles and perform location address lookups using OpenStreetMap Nominatim. No personal data is attached to tile or search requests.

5. Third-Party Services

The App integrates the following third-party service for map visualization:

  • OpenStreetMap & Nominatim: Used for map tiles and place search. For more details on OpenStreetMap's privacy practices, visit OpenStreetMap Privacy Policy.

We do not integrate any advertising SDKs, tracking libraries, or diagnostic profiling tools.

6. Children's Privacy

Fake GPS Location is not directed to individuals under the age of 13. We do not knowingly solicit or collect data from children under 13.

7. Compliance with Google Play Developer Policies

This Privacy Policy complies with Google Play Developer Program Policies, ensuring clear disclosures regarding user data, foreground service location transparency, and local device processing.
