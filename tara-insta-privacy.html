Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 22, 2026

Tara Auto DM ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Tara Auto DM mobile application (the "Service"). This privacy policy outlines how we handle data when you connect and interact with our Service.

1. Information Collection and Use

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We collect and process the following information solely to provide and improve the automated messaging features of the Service:

  • Instagram Account Information: When you connect your Instagram account using Meta OAuth, we temporarily use your public username, profile ID, and access tokens to query media and automate replies.
  • Message and Comment Content: We process comment details (like comment text and commenter username) to verify trigger keywords and send automated DMs containing the links you configured.

2. Data Security

The security of your data is important to us. All access tokens and credentials are encrypted and stored securely within Firebase Firestore. We implement standard industry security practices to prevent unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure of data.

3. Data Deletion and Account Disconnection

You can disconnect your Instagram account from Tara Auto DM at any time through the application settings. Disconnecting your account will immediately revoke all access tokens and remove your connected profile details from our active database.
